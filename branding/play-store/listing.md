Ferry: Private File Transfer

## Full description (max 4000)

Ferry sends files and folders straight from one device to another, even across the internet. No account and no cloud upload.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose files or a folder, or share them to Ferry from any app.
• Ferry makes a ticket: a link and a QR code.
• Send the link however you like, or let someone scan the code. The recipient opens it in Ferry, previews what's inside, and downloads directly from your phone.
• Turn sharing off whenever you want to stop access.

RECEIVING
• Scan the QR code on the sender's screen, paste a link, or open a .ferry ticket file.
• See the file names and sizes before you download.
• Received files are saved to Downloads/Ferry, where your Files app and other apps can find them.
• Interrupted downloads resume where they stopped.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Transfers are end-to-end encrypted between the two devices.
• Every byte is checked against a BLAKE3 hash, so what arrives is exactly what was sent.
• Ferry connects directly when it can. When a network blocks that, an encrypted relay passes the data along, and the relay can't read your files.
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.

WORKS WITH YOUR COMPUTER
Ferry is also available for Windows, macOS and Linux. Send from your phone to your laptop, from your PC to a friend's phone, or between any two devices running Ferry.

GOOD TO KNOW
• The sender's device has to be online with sharing on until the download finishes.
• Treat a ticket like a key: anyone who has it can download while the share is active.

Ferry is free and open source under the MIT license.
