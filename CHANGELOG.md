## 0.1.0

- Share files and folders using a Ferry link, a `.ferry` ticket file, or a QR code.
- Preview a share before downloading. Verify file contents and resume interrupted transfers.
- Transfer directly when possible and use an encrypted relay when needed.
- Send and receive from the desktop app or the command line.
